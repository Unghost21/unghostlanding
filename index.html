01 · The Problem

Both sides lose.

The way hiring works right now is broken in both directions. Talent disappears into résumé floods. Companies drown in noise. We fix the middle.

FOR STUDENTS

Apply everywhere. Hear back from no one.

Endless résumés disappear into ATS black holes. The good ones get filtered out by keyword. The bad ones get the same silence. Drive doesn't show up on a CV.

FOR COMPANIES

Post one role. Drown in résumés.

HR teams spend more time sorting than hiring. Job boards push reach, not fit. By the time a real candidate surfaces, they've already taken a different offer.

02 · The Answer

Unghost is a curated hiring community.

Not a job board
Not an agency
Not another course platform

We train motivated learners, hand-pick them, and personally introduce them to hiring teams. Companies come to us. We pick the candidate. We make the introduction. That's the whole product — and the reason it works.
